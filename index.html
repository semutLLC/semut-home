Why Semut exists

What is a human?

As AI becomes more capable, one question grows more important: what does it mean to be human?

Semut explores this question by building systems that strengthen human agency.

Rather than building separate research and products, Semut weaves them together. Our research informs our tools. Our tools test our ideas.

Today, we focus on helping local businesses coordinate better. Tomorrow, these systems will scale to teams, communities, and organizations.
