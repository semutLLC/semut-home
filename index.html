Why Semut Exists

Semut began with a simple question:

What does it mean to be human in an age of AI?

We believe AI should strengthen human agency rather than replace it.

Our research informs the products we build, and our products continuously test those ideas in the real world.

Today we help local businesses coordinate everyday work. Tomorrow these same coordination systems can scale to teams, communities, and organizations.
